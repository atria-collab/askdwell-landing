10Common questions

The answers to "but what about…"客户常见问题 Q&A

AskDwell is the AI operating layer for property management. It works on top of a company's existing PMS and communication tools, turning everyday conversations and requests into structured workflows that AI can answer, execute, follow through or escalate. It helps property teams manage more doors with a smaller team without replacing their existing systems or removing human control.

AskDwell 是物业管理行业的 AI 运营层。它建立在物业管理公司现有的 PMS 和沟通工具之上，将日常对话和请求转化为结构化工作流，由 AI 直接回答、执行、持续跟进，或在需要时升级给人工处理。它帮助物业团队在不更换现有系统、也不削弱人工控制的前提下，用更精简的团队管理更多房源。

No. Your PMS remains the official system of record for leases, accounting, tenant information and work orders. AskDwell sits above the PMS as an interaction, orchestration and execution layer. It connects through available APIs or authorized data interfaces and can also coordinate work across email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp and other tools. We confirm the exact integration scope during the initial workflow-mapping process.

不会。现有 PMS 仍然是租约、财务、租户信息和工单等正式业务记录的管理系统。AskDwell 位于 PMS 之上，提供交互、流程编排和执行能力。它可以通过现有 API 或获得授权的数据接口进行连接，也可以协调电子邮件、电话、短信、WhatsApp 和其他工具中的工作。具体集成范围会在前期工作流梳理过程中确认。

AskDwell can support workflows across leasing, tenant communication, repair and maintenance, vendor coordination, owner reporting and approvals. Examples include following up with leasing leads, collecting complete maintenance information, coordinating the next steps of a work order, generating customized owner reports and routing higher-risk decisions for approval. We normally recommend starting with one high-value workflow and expanding after the value is proven.

AskDwell 可以支持租赁、租户沟通、维修与维护、供应商协调、业主报告和审批等工作流程。例如，持续跟进租赁线索、收集完整报修信息、协调工单的后续执行、生成定制业主报告，以及将较高风险的决定提交审批。我们通常建议先从一项高价值流程开始，在验证价值后再逐步扩展。

We begin with a focused workflow-mapping session. Together, we identify the repetitive process consuming the most staff time, map the systems, communication channels, staff roles and approval rules involved, and agree on a measurable success metric. AskDwell is then configured for that workflow and tested in the live operating environment. The implementation timeline depends on workflow complexity and the accessibility of the client's existing systems, so we provide a specific plan after discovery.

我们会先进行一次聚焦的工作流梳理。双方共同找出目前最占用员工时间的重复性流程，明确涉及的系统、沟通渠道、员工职责和审批规则，并确定可量化的成功标准。之后，我们会针对该流程配置 AskDwell，并在真实运营环境中进行测试。实施时间取决于流程复杂程度以及现有系统的接口条件，因此我们会在需求调研完成后提供具体实施方案。

The client determines what AskDwell may handle automatically and what still requires human review or approval. Routine, low-risk actions can be automated, while financial, legal, operational or other higher-risk decisions can be routed to the appropriate person. Approval rules can be configured by workflow, action type, property, dollar amount and staff role.

客户可以自行决定哪些工作允许 AskDwell 自动处理，哪些工作仍然需要人工审核或批准。常规、低风险操作可以自动完成；涉及财务、法律、运营或其他较高风险的决定，则可以提交给合适的负责人审批。审批规则可以按照工作流程、操作类型、物业、金额和员工角色进行设置。

AskDwell is designed to ask follow-up questions when information is incomplete and to escalate the task when it does not have sufficient confidence or authority to proceed. The escalation includes the relevant background, source information and recommended next steps so an employee doesn't need to reconstruct the case. Actions and corrections can be recorded for auditability and used to improve the property-specific knowledge and workflow over time.

当信息不完整时，AskDwell 会通过追问补充必要信息；当系统没有足够把握或权限继续执行时，会将任务升级给人工处理。升级任务会同时附带相关背景、信息来源和建议步骤，员工不需要重新梳理整个事件。系统还可以记录操作和人工纠正，便于审计，并持续改进该物业专属的知识和工作流程。

The client retains ownership and control of its property data, documents, communications, operating procedures and customer-specific outputs. AskDwell accesses only the information needed to perform the agreed workflows. We do not sell customer data, share one client's confidential information with another, or use identifiable data to train public or shared AI models without explicit written consent. Wherever possible, sensitive information is de-identified or masked before an AI model processes it — names, phone numbers, emails, banking info and account IDs can be removed or replaced with internal reference IDs. Our security approach is built around encryption, role-based permissions, customer-level data separation, least-privilege access and audit trails. These commitments can be documented in the services agreement and a data-processing or security addendum.

客户始终保留对其物业数据、文件、沟通记录、运营流程和客户专属输出的所有权和控制权。AskDwell 只会访问完成双方约定工作流程所必需的信息。未经客户明确书面同意，我们不会出售客户数据，不会向其他客户提供某一客户的保密信息，也不会使用可识别身份的客户数据训练公开或共享的 AI 模型。在条件允许的情况下，敏感信息会在交由 AI 模型处理之前进行脱敏或屏蔽：姓名、电话、邮箱、银行信息和账户编号可被删除或替换为内部参考编号。我们的安全设计包括数据加密、基于角色的权限、客户间数据隔离、最小权限访问和审计记录。这些承诺可写入服务协议以及数据处理或安全附录。

Option 2 — lower base fee + usage-based: you pay based on actual tokens consumed, so lighter usage costs less. For an initial pilot we can agree on a clear budget or usage cap so cost stays predictable.

由于 AskDwell 目前仍处于早期阶段，我们在定价方式上比较灵活，目前可以考虑两种收费结构。方案一 — 按户订阅：目前预计约为每户每月 2 至 4 美元，具体取决于管理规模、集成要求和工作流范围；现阶段这项费用主要用于覆盖工程开发、实施和持续支持成本。方案二 — 较低基础费 + 按量计费：客户根据实际消耗的 AI 资源付费，使用量少费用也低。对于初始试点，双方可预先约定明确预算或使用上限，使费用保持可预测。

Before launch, we agree on a baseline and a small set of success metrics for the selected workflow. Depending on the use case these may include response time, staff hours per task, maintenance resolution time, leasing conversion, requests resolved without manual coordination, owner or tenant satisfaction, and operating cost per unit. The pilot's purpose is to demonstrate measurable operational value before expanding.

在正式启动之前，双方会针对所选择的工作流确定现状基准和一组核心成功指标。根据具体场景，这些指标可以包括回复速度、每项任务所需人工时间、维修解决时间、租赁转化率、无需人工协调即可解决的请求数量、业主或租户满意度，以及单套房源运营成本。试点项目的目的，就是在扩大使用范围之前，先证明可量化的运营价值。

The required access depends on the workflow. For a reporting workflow, AskDwell may need read-only access to selected property and operating data. For a maintenance workflow, it may also need permission to create or update tasks, send approved communications and record status changes. We follow a minimum-access approach and define the permitted data, actions and user roles before implementation.

所需访问权限取决于具体工作流。对于报告流程，AskDwell 可能只需要读取指定的物业和运营数据。对于维修流程，系统可能还需要创建或更新任务、发送已获授权的沟通信息，以及记录状态变化。我们遵循最小权限原则，并在实施前明确允许访问的数据、允许执行的操作和对应用户角色。

Yes. Each property or portfolio can have different SOPs, approval limits, staff roles, communication preferences, vendor relationships and reporting requirements. AskDwell applies those property-specific rules while reusing proven workflow templates. Once a workflow is validated, it can be adapted and deployed across additional properties without rebuilding the entire process from scratch.

可以。每处物业或每个投资组合都可以拥有不同的 SOP、审批额度、员工角色、沟通偏好、供应商关系和报告要求。AskDwell 可以执行这些物业专属规则，同时复用已经验证的工作流模板。一项流程验证成功后，可以针对更多物业进行调整和部署，而不需要从头重新开发整个流程。

During implementation we map the workflow, configure integrations and approval rules, test real cases and monitor the initial rollout. 在实施过程中，我们会与客户共同梳理工作流程，配置系统集成和审批规则，测试真实案例，并监控初始上线情况。持续支持可以包括工作流调整、问题解决和效果复盘。客户始终拥有自己的数据。如果双方终止合作，客户可以导出其客户专属信息，并根据合同约定的数据保留、备份和法律要求提出删除请求。AskDwell 的底层软件和通用工作流技术仍属于 AskDwell 的知识产权。
