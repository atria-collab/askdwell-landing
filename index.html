Colby · tenants

Meets tenants where they already are

No app to download, no portal to learn. Colby talks to residents on the channels they use — and remembers their preferences across conversations.

  • WhatsApp, SMS & Telegram, 24/7
  • Answers in the tenant's own language
  • Scoped to just their home — never another unit or ledger

Colby · maintenance

Turns a vague complaint into a clean work order

Colby describes the issue back to confirm it, checks for duplicates, and drops anything that sounds unsafe straight to a human — then files it.

  • Asks for photos and the details staff actually need
  • Offers a safe self-fix first — and files nothing if it works
  • Creates the work order in Buildium with a reference number

Maven · staff

Answers your team across the whole portfolio

Maven reads everything staff need — tenants, leases, money, work orders, adoption — right inside your team's channel.

  • Any tenant, lease, or unit by name or address
  • What a lease owes and its exact payment history
  • What similar jobs actually cost — averages, medians

Maven · the write surface

Nothing changes until a person says go

Every action Maven can take is a draft. A manager reads the exact wording and values, approves, and only then does it happen — and it's all logged.

  • Draft → a manager approves → it happens → it's logged
  • Never invents a confirmation it can't back up
  • Adding staff needs two different managers to sign off
assign the sink leak to Dave, bump priority
Draft → assign #55501 to Dave Ruiz, priority High. Approve to apply?
approve ✅
Done. #55501 → Dave Ruiz, High. Logged: you, 10:42 AM.
Colby · lease & rules

Answers from the actual lease — not a generic policy

Pet rules, sublet terms, quiet hours, late fees — Colby searches that market's real lease document, clause by clause, scoped to the tenant asking.

  • Dates, rent, deposit, who's on the lease
  • Pets, parking, sublet, quiet hours, late fees
  • Cites the tenant's own lease, not a template
